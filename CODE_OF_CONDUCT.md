# Code of Conduct

We want ArkaControls to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient; give and accept constructive feedback; assume good intent; focus on the work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language or jokes, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, sustained disruption, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Report concerns privately through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, which we adopt by reference.
