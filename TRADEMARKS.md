# Trademarks and naming

"ArkaControls", the ArkaControls logo and the `Arka*` package and namespace names are trademarks of DataHub Software.

The Apache License 2.0 (Section 6) does **not** grant permission to use these names, and this policy is how we apply that.

## You may

- Use the software, modify it and redistribute it under the licence, keeping the copyright notices, the `LICENSE` file and the `NOTICE` file.
- Say truthfully that your product **uses**, is **built with** or is **based on** ArkaControls, and link to https://github.com/DataHub-Software/ArkaControls.
- Refer to ArkaControls by name in articles, tutorials, issues and package descriptions where it is needed to describe compatibility.

## You may not

- Publish a fork, package or product under the name ArkaControls, or under a name likely to be confused with it, or use our logo.
- Remove or alter copyright headers, `LICENSE` or `NOTICE`, or present the code as originally written by someone else.
- Suggest that DataHub Software sponsors, endorses or supports your fork or product without written permission.

A fork must choose its own name and namespace and state clearly that it is a fork of ArkaControls.

## Reporting misuse

Open an issue titled "Trademark or attribution concern" or use the private contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

_This page is a project policy, not legal advice. Trademark rights are strongest when the mark is registered in the countries where the software is distributed; registration is a step for the owner to take with a lawyer._
